# Candidates — One Line, One Laugh

Judge brief (from RULES.txt): one hilarious line, max 200 characters, lands
on a first read. Weighted: surprise (the turn is the joke) > economy >
freshness > clean wording. Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, no ties.

Avoided themes already used by our own earlier one-line entries: AI
assistants / coding agents doing the job, cats, warning labels, office
jokes, AI confessions, dating bios, robot wedding vows. All lines below are
written from scratch for this bounty.

1. I bought a standing desk to be healthier; now I sit on it. (58)
2. My Roomba got stuck under the couch, so now I pay a cleaner to rescue the cleaner. (84)
3. My phone's screen-time report called me brave for opening the same app 214 times. (82)
4. My calendar reminded me to relax, so I scheduled a meeting to discuss it. (76)
5. I keep a list of things to do tomorrow — it's the only thing I've finished every day for six years. (98)
6. My GPS said "recalculating" in the exact tone my mother uses when I disappoint her. (83)
7. I told my smart speaker I was lonely; it scheduled me a meeting with myself. (79)
8. My bank flagged my $4 coffee as suspicious but called my $900 flight to nowhere "consistent with my spending". (113)
9. I asked AI to write my wedding vows; it proposed to my laptop. (61)
10. My smartwatch congratulated me for standing up, which is the saddest applause I've ever received. (99)

## Pick: #1

"I bought a standing desk to be healthier; now I sit on it."

Why it wins the brief:
- Surprise: the setup promises virtue (healthier), the punchline inverts it
  completely in five words — the desk's whole purpose defeated by its owner.
- Economy: 58 characters, every word earns its place; no setup longer than
  the punchline needs. The shortest finalist.
- Freshness: original phrasing of an everyday object; not a stock pun, not a
  restated saying, and no AI/cat/office theme we have already entered.
- Clean wording: plain grammar, reads smoothly aloud, one breath.

Why the others lost:
- #2 (Roomba) is the runner-up — same inversion shape, but "rescue the
  cleaner" needs a beat longer to land and costs 26 more characters.
- #3 (screen time) is relatable but its turn ("brave") is a compliment
  twist, softer than a purpose-defeat.
- #4 (calendar) is strong but the "meeting about relaxing" beat is a more
  familiar productivity joke.
- #5, #6, #7, #8, #10 are good lines with real turns, but each spends more
  words for a smaller inversion than #1.
- #9 reuses the AI-assistant theme our earlier entries already mined.
